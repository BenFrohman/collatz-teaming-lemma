\begin{abstract}
We formalize in Lean 4 (\texttt{CollatzTeaming}, 23 modules) the inverse-residue ``teaming'' architecture for the classical Collatz map $T$, proving the logical equivalence \texttt{RemainingLemma $\leftrightarrow$ CollatzConjecture}, certifying the trivial cycle $C_0 = (1 \to 4 \to 2 \to 1)$, inverse parent rules, finite orbit scene certificates, and the $M_{92}$ block-map Diophantine identities, while leaving the universal \texttt{remaining\_lemma} explicitly open. In companion notes (\texttt{MU\_F\_SLOGAN\_NOTE.md}, \texttt{ELIAHOU\_WINDOWS.md}, \texttt{RHIN\_DERIVATION.md}), we establish the Frohman Model Mean observation: the Syracuse logarithmic drift $\mu_F = \log(3/4) < 0$ is identical for $3n+1$ and $3n-1$, yet $3n-1$ possesses three distinct positive cycles ($1$, $5$, and $17$). Thus negative expected logarithmic drift alone cannot rule out non-trivial cycles.
\end{abstract}

\section{Certified Kernel Components vs.\ Open Frontier}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Certified in Lean 4:} The classical map $T$, trivial cycle $C_0 = \{1,4,2\}$, dichotomy/reduction predicates $D$ and $R$, inverse-residue team definitions, \texttt{RemainingLemma $\leftrightarrow$ CollatzConjecture}, $M_{92}$ block-map identities, and Eliahou/Steiner/Rhin structural reductions.
  \item \textbf{Frohman Model Mean Observation ($\mu_F = \log(3/4) < 0$):} Demonstrates via the conjugate map $C_-(n) = 3n-1$ (equivalent to $3n+1$ on negative integers) that negative heuristic drift coexists with multiple periodic attractors ($1 \to 2 \to 1$, the 5-cycle at $5$, and the 18-step cycle at $17$).
  \item \textbf{Explicit Non-Claim:} No proof of the global Collatz conjecture is claimed; \texttt{remaining\_lemma} is explicitly marked open.
\end{enumerate}
