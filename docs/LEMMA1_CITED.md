# Lemma 1 — statement and citations

## What Lemma 1 actually says

Every closed T-orbit is C_0. Blocks are paths; an m-cycle is m such paths glued until they close. If they do not close, there is no cycle.

## What is proved (cite, do not redo)

Steiner 1977: only 1-cycle is trivial (n(2^L-3)=1 ⇒ n=1, L=2). That is this project’s Proposition 1.1.

- R. P. Steiner, A theorem on the Syracuse problem, in Proceedings of the Seventh Manitoba Conference on Numerical Mathematics and Computing (Winnipeg, 1977), Congressus Numerantium 20, Utilitas Math., Winnipeg, 1978, pp. 553–559. MR535032.

Simons 2005 / 2007: no 2-cycle (Diophantine system + log_2 3 convergents + linear forms in logarithms). The a,b ≤ 30 grind is obsolete as a 2-cycle statement.

- J. L. Simons, On the nonexistence of 2-cycles for the 3x+1 problem, Mathematics of Computation 74 (2005), 1565–1572. DOI 10.1090/S0025-5718-04-01728-4.
- J. L. Simons, A simple (inductive) proof for the non-existence of 2-cycles of the 3x+1 problem, Journal of Number Theory 123 (2007), 10–17. DOI 10.1016/j.jnt.2006.05.011.

Simons–de Weger: no m-cycle through m = 68; later updates with a stronger verified range raise that floor (76 in their 2010 note).

- J. L. Simons and B. M. M. de Weger, Theoretical and computational bounds for m-cycles of the 3n+1 problem, Acta Arithmetica 117 (2005), 51–70.

Hercher 2022/23: no m-cycle for m ≤ 91.

- C. Hercher, There are no Collatz m-cycles with m ≤ 91, arXiv:2201.00406v3 [math.NT], 4 April 2023.

Eliahou 1993: any nontrivial period is of the form 301994a + 17087915b + 85137581c (b ≥ 1, ac = 0). The fraction 301994/190537 is a continued-fraction window, not a cycle.

- S. Eliahou, The 3x+1 problem: new lower bounds on nontrivial cycle lengths, Discrete Mathematics 118 (1993), 45–56. DOI 10.1016/0012-365X(93)90052-U.

These citations support partial bans on m-cycles and a constraint on period shape. They do not prove Lemma 1.
