- expected number of divisions by 2 before the next odd is two;
- next odd expected about 3n/4 on average;
- average size of odd terms decreases toward 1;
- the package is a heuristic that predicts the conjecture, and is non-rigorous.

Do not write “Tao’s model mean” or “Tao’s Lyapunov.”
