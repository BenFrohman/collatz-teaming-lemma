# Do not misread the μ_F note

Claimed:

    ¬( μ_F < 0  ⇒  unique basin {1} ).

Witness for that only: C_- has three attractors and 5 never goes to 1.
Wrong map for L and for Lemma 1. Extra cycles of C_- sit inside L_-, they do not negate it.

If someone writes ¬L, ¬L_-, or ¬Lemma 1 from this file, that reading is false.
Those three statements are not theorems here and are not corollaries of the slogan disproof.
