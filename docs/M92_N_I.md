## What n_i is

A 92-cycle is 92 odd local minima

    n_1, n_2, …, n_92

each positive odd, together with widths (k_i, ℓ_i), satisfying

    2^{k_i+ℓ_i} n_{i+1} + 2^{k_i} = 3^{k_i} (n_i + 1),   i=1,…,92,
    n_93 = n_1 > 1.

Simons form: n_i = a_i 2^{k_i} − 1 with a_i odd positive.
Residue: k_i ≥ 2 forces n_i ≡ 3 (mod 4).

## What is written

    n_1 = 1

with (k,ℓ)=(1,1) closes at n'=1. That is C_0. It fails n_1 > 1 as a nontrivial cycle.

No list

    (n_1, n_2, …, n_92)

of positive odds with n_93 = n_1 > 1 is written. ExistsNontrivial92 stays uninhabited.
