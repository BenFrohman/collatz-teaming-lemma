# Scope of the μ_F disproof

Disproved.

    μ_F < 0  ⇒  every orbit goes to 1

and the coarser form

    negative expected drift  ⇒  exactly one attractor.

Witness: C_- has the same large-n average log(3/4) and already has three attractors. 5 never goes to 1.

Not disproved.

- Lemma L for T=3n+1. C_- is the wrong map.
- Lemma L_-. Extra cycles of C_- are the target of L_-, not a fourth cycle.
- Lemma 1 / Δ_cyc(T)=∅.

This kills a slogan about μ_F. It does not settle Collatz.
