Locked. That is the record shape.

An m-cycle is m blocks

    (odd ascent)^{k_i} then (even descent)^{\ell_i},    i = 1, …, m,

glued until n_{m+1} = n_1. If they do not close, it is not a cycle.

- m = number of those blocks = number of local minima. Not K.
- First unbanned block-count: m = 92 (Hercher Thm. 23). No later paper raises that floor.
- No such loop is written. “Record” means the constraints an unwritten 92-block loop would have to obey.

Constraints on that unwritten loop:

- Cor. 24: if m ≤ 98 then K > 7.76 × 10^{19}.
- Cor. 29 under Barina: K > 1.375 × 10^{11} for any m.

The first iterate of option 2 (rerun at m=92, X0=2^{71}) is in docs/M92_ITERATION.md. It does not ban m=92.
