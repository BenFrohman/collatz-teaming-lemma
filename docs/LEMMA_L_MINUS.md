# Lemma L_-

Right map:

    C_-(n) = n/2 if n even, else 3n-1.

Not T=3n+1. Not μ_F. μ_F = log(3/4) is the expected-contraction heuristic for T, aimed at the single attractor C0. That is the wrong target for L_-.

Target set:

    I   = (1 → 2 → 1)
    II  = (5 → 14 → 7 → 20 → 10 → 5)
    III = (17-cycle, 18 terms)

Lemma L_- := every n>0 has C_--orbit meeting I ∪ II ∪ III.

The 5-cycle and the 17-cycle are not defects in the statement. They are two of the three sets L_- says catch every positive start.

A fourth C_- cycle would kill L_-. An unbounded C_- orbit would kill L_-. Those extra cycles do not kill L_-.

What would close L_-: a proof, for every n>0, that the C_- orbit meets I ∪ II ∪ III. A prefix through 10^3 or 10^4 is not that proof. μ_F is not that proof.
