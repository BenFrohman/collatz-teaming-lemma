# Lemma L_- — C_- analogue of L

Map: C_-(n) = n/2 if even, else 3n-1.
Target set: the three known positive cycles

    C_-^I   = (1 → 2 → 1)
    C_-^II  = (5 → 14 → 7 → 20 → 10 → 5)
    C_-^III = (17-cycle, 18 terms).

Lemma L_- := every n>0 eventually lands in C_-^I ∪ C_-^II ∪ C_-^III.

This is not Lemma L for T=3n+1. The three cycles are not Δ_cyc(T).

Status: open. Prefix searches (including N=10,000 in the census) do not prove it. A further C_- cycle would refute L_-. Divergence of a C_- orbit would refute L_-.
