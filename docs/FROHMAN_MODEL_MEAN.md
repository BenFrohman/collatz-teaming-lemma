# Frohman model mean

Name on this ledger for the expected odd-to-odd contraction of T=3n+1:

    μ_F := log(3/4) < 0.

That is the log of the expected next odd term 3n/4 after two expected halvings.

Tao 2020 calls the same calculation a “convincing (but non-rigorous) heuristic argument.” He does not call it a model mean and does not call it a Lyapunov. The quantity is classical. The ledger name is Frohman model mean.

## What it is

An expected log-drift under the heuristic that v_2(3n+1) is distributed as a geometric law with mean 2. It supports Collatz as a heuristic. It is not Lemma 2. It is not Lemma 2_V.

## What it is not

- Not a Lyapunov. V(n)=log n rises at 27 --v=1--> 41.
- Not a proof that Δ_div=∅.
- Not Tao’s title for the argument.

Realized means on the orbit of 27 (after it hits C0) are not μ_F and are not a V.
