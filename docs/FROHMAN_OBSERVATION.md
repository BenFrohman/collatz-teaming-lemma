# Frohman observation (not a new cycle)

Statement:

    On C_- the large-n average is still log(3/4), and two extra cycles already exist.
    So μ_F < 0 does not mean one attractor.
    Cycle odd-to-odd log-sum 0 is not a contradiction; it is the definition of a cycle.

That is the usable point. It is a constraint on μ_F, not a newly found periodic orbit.

## What is not named Frohman

These are classical C_- cycles (Lagarias / Tao notes / the census):

    I   1 → 2 → 1
    II  5 → 14 → 7 → 20 → 10 → 5
    III 17-cycle, 18 terms

They are not Frohman cycles. Priority for those loops is not claimed here.

## What may be called the Frohman comparison orbit

The same start on two maps:

    T:    27 → 82 → 41 → … → C0     (v=1 first odd-to-odd)
    C_-:  27 → 80 → 40 → 20 → 10 → 5 → cycle II

That pair is the ledger example that μ_F is map-dependent in its conclusion (one attractor vs three) even when the large-n average looks the same. It is still the classical start 27, not a new cycle.
