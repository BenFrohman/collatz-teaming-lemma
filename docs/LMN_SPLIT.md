# LMN split (typed)

Let LMN be Laurent–Mignotte–Nesterenko, Formes linéaires en deux logarithmes et déterminants d’interpolation, J. Number Theory 55 (1995), 285–321.

Kind A (proved for each fixed m that the literature has closed).
Input: a fixed block-count m, the cycle equation that forces Λ = L log 2 − K log 3 small, an LMN lower bound on |Λ|, a growth lower bound on K, and a verified range for small starts.
Output: no m-cycle. Steiner m=1, Simons m=2, Simons–de Weger through m=68 (later 76), Hercher through m=91.
LMN is the reason Kind A terminates.

Kind B (Lemma 1).
Output: ∀ m, no m-cycle except C0. Equivalent: Δ_cyc(T)=∅.
Kind A at m=91 does not imply Kind B. Kind B needs every remaining m, or a different argument that does not stop at a finite m.

Not Kind A and not Kind B: μ_F, the a,b ≤ 30 grind, C_- extra cycles, realized log-mean on 27.

Status: Kind A through m=91 is literature. Kind B is open.
