# Positive cycles of C_- = 3n-1

These are cycles of a different map. They are not Δ_cyc(T) for T=3n+1.
Conjugacy: C_{-c}(n) = -C_c(-n). So these three are the three known negative 3n+1 cycles with signs flipped.

## Cycle I — the 1-cycle

    1 → 2 → 1

Odds: 1. Full length 2.

## Cycle II — the 5-cycle

    5 → 14 → 7 → 20 → 10 → 5

Odds: 5, 7. Full length 5.

## Cycle III — the 17-cycle

    17 → 50 → 25 → 74 → 37 → 110 → 55 → 164 → 82
      → 41 → 122 → 61 → 182 → 91 → 272 → 136 → 68 → 34 → 17

Odds: 17, 25, 37, 55, 41, 61, 91. Full length 18.

## Status

Known and classical (Lagarias; Tao notes). Your census of starts 1..10^4 sees exactly these three. It is not proved that they are all the positive cycles of C_-. It is not proved that every positive C_- orbit reaches one of them.

They stay out of Lemma 1 for T.
