# Contributing

Author and copyright holder: Benjamin Stanley Frohman.

## Approval

- Do not merge, rebase onto main, force-push, squash, or retitle this work as a Collatz proof without the author's explicit written approval.
- Open a pull request. Wait for review from @BenFrohman. Unsolicited commits to main will be reverted.
- Forks must keep the copyright line, LICENSE, and the statement that the remaining lemma is not certified.

## Content rules

- Do not delete `sorry` on `remaining_lemma` unless a complete proof is supplied and the author accepts it.
- Do not write ¬L, ¬L_-, or ¬Lemma 1 from the μ_F slogan note.
- Do not rename the classical C_- 5-cycle or 17-cycle as a Frohman cycle.
- Cite Steiner, Simons, Simons–de Weger, Hercher, Eliahou by the entries in docs/LEMMA1_CITED.md.
